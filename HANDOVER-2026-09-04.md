## The lesson of the day, and it is not a new one

Yesterday: *a step that succeeds and cannot record its success is invisible.* Today produced five
more of the same family, and the sharpest version is:

> **Almost every fault today came from shipping something without exercising it once.**

The demo-Microsoft leak, two review gates nothing could satisfy, a backoff written into a `date`
column so it never backed off, telling Felix his decisions had not landed when they had. Each would
have been caught by running the thing a single time. The pieces that were proved by firing —
the alarms, the release path, the lock — have all held.

⚠️ **A page that says the machine is unfinished when it has found a fact about the company.** Three
separate strings did this today: "Not yet scored" on the public site, "Not decided yet" on
categories nobody was being asked about, and "1 unresolved" on a gap Felix had already decided.
The page defaults to blaming itself and the default is usually wrong.
