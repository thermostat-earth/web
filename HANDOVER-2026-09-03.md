## The one live decision

H&M reports capital goods as **N/A in every year**, with the sentence "No activities with
relevant climate impact have been identified for categories 2, 8, 9, 10, and 13". Its own
consolidated cash flow statement for the same year shows **SEK 10,679 m** spent acquiring
capital assets. An immateriality argument might have been available; an absence-of-activity
one is not, and H&M makes the second.

Scoring requires every applicable category to carry a figure, so this single gap rejects
**all six** of H&M's years and holds the score. Once it is settled, `unlock_and_score`
publishes on the 2022–2025 window.

## The real finding of the day: the joins, not the steps

Every step 0–7 runs and does its job. **Four separate failures were found, and all four were
in the handover between steps.** All four were silent — the step ran, produced the correct
answer, and the answer went nowhere. Nothing alerted. Each was found only by going and looking.

| | What ran correctly | Where the answer went |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Restatement reasons were read and staged | `commit-company.mjs` was missing `restatement_reason` from its scope-3 insert — present in the scope-12 insert directly above it. Every scope-3 reason ever staged was dropped. |
| 2 | `score_company` knew a missing category was the cause | Its own "name the cause, not the symptom" block sat *after* the empty-window early exit, so it fired when a category cost *some* years and never when it cost *all* of them. H&M would have published "no scope 1 and 2 data" while holding five years of it. |
| 3 | The Internet Archive fallback reached three Chanel documents | `plan-documents.mjs` printed them as reachable, refreshed `http_status` to 200 and `is_pdf` to true, and left `decision = 'unusable'` sitting beside those two facts. Chanel had been stuck for weeks. |
| 4 | — | The downstream traceability field was a type and some styling that nothing ever wrote. |

All four are fixed. The pattern is what matters:

> **A step that succeeds and cannot record its success is invisible by construction.**
> Nothing fails, nothing warns, and the work looks done.

**This is the thing to design against before step 11 automates anything.** Each join needs a
check that *fails* when the handover does not happen — the shape of the new restatement gate,
which refuses rather than warns. Automating silent joins would only make the silence faster.
