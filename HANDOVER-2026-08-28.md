The question left on the table, unanswered:

H&M's 2022 category 1 is described as *"Expanded supplier traceability including full tier 1 and 2
suppliers"* and moves **+1,952,000 tCO₂e, +38.5%**. Is improved visibility better measurement, or a
wider boundary?

- **Better measurement** — the emissions always existed, the company could not see them. Generous;
  keeps companies scoreable while they improve.
- **Boundary change** — what they count has changed, and 38.5% dwarfs any real decarbonisation in
  that period. Strict; consistent with "a fall in the line must mean a fall in emissions". Would
  break H&M's run at 2022.
- **Split by size** — refinement below a threshold, boundary change above it.

Claude's instinct was the second. Felix had not answered when the day ended.
